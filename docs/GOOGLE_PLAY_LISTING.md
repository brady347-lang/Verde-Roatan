## Full description (English)
Verde Roatán is your plant nursery and landscaping partner on Roatán, Honduras.

• Shop plants and garden products from local nurseries and suppliers, priced in lempiras and dollars.
• Identify a plant, or diagnose a pest or leaf problem, from a photo. Results are suggestions and are labeled as such.
• Search an approved plant knowledge base built from university and extension sources, with the document and page shown for every answer.
• Request landscaping help and receive a quote.
• Choose your delivery area and order with WhatsApp support.
• English and Spanish.

Identification results are estimates, not scientific verification. Always read pesticide labels, and check toxicity before using plants around pets or children.
