# Privacy Policy – Verde Roatán
_Last updated: November 7, 2026. DRAFT: owner must review, add business contact details, and host it at a public URL._

Verde Roatán ("we") is a plant nursery and landscaping app for Roatán, Honduras.

**What we collect:** account email and password (via Supabase Auth); name, phone/WhatsApp, delivery neighborhood and order details you enter; photos you choose to submit for plant identification; your feedback on identification results; basic app usage needed to run orders.

**How we use it:** to create your account, process and deliver orders, identify plants, improve our plant knowledge base, and contact you about your orders.

**Plant photos:** a photo you scan is sent to Google's Gemini API to identify the plant. Identification is an estimate, not a scientific verification.

**Sharing:** we do not sell personal data. Data is processed by Supabase (database/auth), Cloudflare (hosting/API) and Google (Gemini). Suppliers see only the specimens requested and the delivery neighborhood, not your contact details.

**Security:** data is encrypted in transit; access is restricted by database security rules.

**Your rights:** you can ask us to access, correct or delete your account and data at any time: [CONTACT EMAIL]. Deletion requests are handled within 30 days.

**Children:** the app is not directed to children under 13.
